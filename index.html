The Last Bus to Eket
Short Fiction • Complete Story

The yellow engine of the coaster bus sputtered like a dying man trying to catch his final breath under the neon floodlights of Plaza Motor Park. It was 9:45 PM. Rain whipped against the rusted roof in rhythmic pulses, painting the mud outside into a slick black soup.

Ekemini tightened his grip on the leather briefcase sitting across his knees. Inside were three things: five million Naira in unmarked bills, a passport bearing a name that was not his, and a silver ring he had stolen from his uncle's desk two hours ago.

The driver—a burly man smelling of kola nut and dry gin—slammed the side door shut. "Eket! Last bus! If you never enter, stay Plaza till morning!"

A woman sitting beside Ekemini wrapped her child in a damp wrapper and leaned over. "My son," she whispered, her eyes dark with a fear that matched his own. "Did you see the military checkpoint near the bridge on your way here?"

Ekemini’s heart skipped. "No. What checkpoint?"

"They are looking for someone," she replied, pulling her child closer. "They said a boy ran away with the Chief’s money."

As the bus rumbled into the stormy darkness, Ekemini looked back through the fogged rear glass. Two black SUVs had just rolled into the park with their headlights off.

Episode 1 - The Empty House: Anini wakes up in a luxury mansion in Uyo with total amnesia. The staff call her Mrs. Bassey, but every photograph on the wall features another woman's face.

Episode 2 - Ghost in the Registry: Anini visits the local marriage registry to verify her marriage certificate. To her horror, the records state that Mrs. Anini Bassey died in a boat accident three years prior.

Episode 3 - The Second Phone: Hidden inside a hollowed-out book in her master bedroom, Anini discovers a burner phone. It receives a text message: "Don't drink the tea he gives you tonight."
